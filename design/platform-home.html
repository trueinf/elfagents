How it reasoned — step by step
1
Received from e.l.f.orchestra

Launch context for LAUNCH-1001

The orchestrator dispatched the launch with its SKU, formula, and three target markets — US, DE, UK. The agent's job is scoped to legal clearance only; it does not look at supply, artwork, or retail. It reasons over each market independently.

2
Called its tools · deterministic lookups against the warehouse

Pulled ingredient limits and notification status per market

These are tools, not reasoning — each returns a fact from the data. The agent issued six calls across the three markets:

get_ingredient_restrictions(sku, "DE")→Ascorbic Acid · Annex III · limit 20%34ms
get_ingredient_restrictions(sku, "UK")→Ascorbic Acid · limit 25%28ms
get_ingredient_restrictions(sku, "US")→no federal concentration limit22ms
get_notification_status(sku, "DE")→CPNP · in_progress19ms
get_notification_status(sku, "UK")→SCPN · complete18ms
get_notification_status(sku, "US")→not required (MoCRA listing)17ms
3
Reasoned over the facts · this is the judgment, not a lookup

Weighed each market against two independent gates

The agent compared the SKU's actual concentration (22%) against each market's limit, and checked whether the pre-market notification was complete. Crucially, it applied the Annex III nuance: an exceedance here is a conditional restriction (fixable by reformulation), not an outright ban — so it's a hard gate for this formula, but not a permanent prohibition.

MarketIngredient gateNotification gateVerdict
US22% · no limitnot requiredclear

⚖
This is the reasoning a lookup can't do: recognizing that the same 22% is compliant in the UK and blocking in Germany, and that the German block is conditional (reformulate) rather than absolute. That judgment is why this is an agent, not a tool.
4
Emitted a typed finding back to e.l.f.orchestra

Returned a schema-validated RegulatoryFinding

The reasoning is compressed into a typed object the orchestrator can trust — no free-form prose crosses the boundary. The per_market array and hard_gate_markets are what let the orchestrator reconcile against the other three specialists.
